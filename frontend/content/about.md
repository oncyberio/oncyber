# What is Oncyber?

Oncyber is a place on the web where people make and visit 3D spaces. A
space can be a gallery for your art, or a world you build yourself. Every
space has its own link, and anyone who opens the link walks straight in,
unless its creator has put a password or a gate on it.

## What is Oncyber?

Oncyber is a web app for building and visiting 3D spaces. It has gallery spaces for showing art and collections, worlds people build in the browser, and a shared lobby where visitors meet and travel between spaces with the same identity and avatar. It is open source.

## What can I do in a space?

Walk around it, look at the works on show and read about each one, meet the other people who are there, chat, talk by voice, and step through portals into other spaces.

## What can I show in a space?

Images, videos, 3D models, text and embedded pages. Works you own in a connected wallet (NFTs) can be placed as well, with their artist and collection shown beside them.

## Can I use Oncyber as a virtual art gallery?

Yes. Most spaces are exactly that: a 3D gallery people walk through in the browser. Pick a gallery in the [Studio](/studio), hang your images, videos and 3D works, add a text beside each one, and share the link. Visitors need no account and nothing to install.

## Can I hold an online exhibition or an opening?

Yes. Everyone who has the space open at the same time is in it together: they see each other's avatars, chat and talk by voice. Share one link for the show, and connect it to your other spaces with portals.

## Do I need to install anything?

No. Oncyber runs in the browser, on a computer or a phone. It draws with WebGPU where the browser has it and falls back to WebGL where it doesn't. Spaces also open in VR headsets through the browser.

## Is Oncyber free?

Yes. Making a space, publishing it and visiting spaces are free.

## How do I make a space?

Open the [Studio](/studio), pick a gallery or a world to start from, place your works, give the space a name and publish it. Its link is ready to share as soon as it is published.

## Where is my space on the web?

At its own link: `oncyber.com/<your-link>` once you choose one, or `oncyber.com/spaces/<id>` before that. Your profile, with every public space you have made, is at `oncyber.com/@<your-name>`.

## Which wallets work with Oncyber?

Ethereum, Solana and Tezos wallets. A wallet is only needed to place works you own in it; you can sign in and build without one.

## What is the lobby?

The lobby is the shared world everyone arrives in. You see who is around, chat and talk, and travel together into any space.

## What is my avatar?

Your avatar is the 3D character you take everywhere: the lobby, every space, and the trip between them. Pick a built-in one, upload your own, or wear one you own as an NFT. See [Avatars](/docs/avatars).

## Can I bring a world I built with another engine?

That is where Oncyber is going: any 3D world on the web, made with any engine, becoming a space. See [Bring your own world](/docs/three-experiences).

## Is Oncyber open source?

Yes. The code is at [github.com/oncyberio/oncyber](https://github.com/oncyberio/oncyber). You can run it, read it and change it; see [Run Oncyber locally](/docs/run-locally) and [Contribute](/docs/contributing).
