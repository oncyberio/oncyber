# Bring your own world

Any 3D world on the web, made with three.js, WebGPU or any other engine,
should be able to become a space in Oncyber. That's what we're building
towards.

## The idea

-   **Your engine, your files.** You build your world however you like and keep
    it wherever you like. Oncyber never needs to import your engine.
-   **Oncyber brings the people.** Visitors walk in from the lobby with the
    same identity, avatar and friends they have everywhere else in Oncyber, and
    keep chatting and talking while they're in your world.
-   **One way to move.** Worlds feel like the rest of Oncyber: the same keys to
    walk, run, jump and look around, and the same menu to head back out.
-   **Safe by default.** Your world runs in a sandbox. It never touches a
    visitor's account or wallet; anything sensitive goes through Oncyber, which
    asks the visitor first, every time.

## Help build it

This is open work. If you want to help, start with
[Contribute](/docs/contributing), and see [Multiplayer](/docs/multiplayer) and
[Avatars](/docs/avatars) for how identity and presence work in Oncyber's own
spaces.
