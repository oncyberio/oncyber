    Shift to walk, Space to jump. Drag to look, scroll to zoom.

    E
    Pick up · use

    Pick up bloomlings, fruit and critters. Near a tree, shake it. Holding food? E feeds the bloomling in front of you.

    F
    Pet

    Hold to pet. Petted bloomlings grow up Sunny. Pet eggs to hatch them sooner.

    Q
    Throw

    Toss what you hold. A little play is fine; lots of tossing raises a cheeky Shade.

    R
    Whistle

    Your bloomlings come running.

    Tab
    Your bloomlings

    Stats, hunger and mood. Click one in the world too.

    Fruit: Puffberries fill tummies and build stamina. Sun Peaches and Moon Plums nudge a bloomling toward Sunny or Shade.

    Critters: give a Hopper, Tweet, Fishlet, Digger or Shellby to a bloomling: its stat jumps and it grows ears, wings, fins, paws or a shell.

    Mini games: Bloom Dash at the arch on the east beach, Bump Dojo in the cliff cave. Other players can join.
