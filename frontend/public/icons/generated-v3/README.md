## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one production UI icon for the Oncyber "Jump In" button, displayed at only 22 pixels. Generate a single finished icon, not a contact sheet or mockup.
Primary request: a running human passing THROUGH one tilted portal ring from left to right. One continuous runner straddles the portal: the rear arm and rear leg remain on the LEFT of the ellipse; the forward-facing outlined head, leading arm and bent forward leg emerge on its RIGHT. The portal plane crosses the middle of the runner, with a tiny transparent interruption at the crossing to clarify what is in front. Exactly one human and one ring.
Style: bold, minimal, friendly orbital line-art icon, like our substantial rounded Saturn-ring iconography. The portal is an upright elliptical hoop slanted forward about 18 degrees, giving it an italic, dimensional feel. Dynamic readable running pose, rounded line ends, consistent substantial stroke weight. Hollow circular head, never a filled head. No facial detail. Ring and body must both be extremely clear at small button scale.
Composition: compact centered near-square symbol filling about 84% of canvas, balanced margins. Entire icon fits safely inside canvas. The body extends on BOTH sides of the portal so it reads as crossing into a 3D world.
Color: pure solid WHITE strokes only, with a genuinely transparent background and transparent interiors. Clean antialiased edges. No grey, no colored pixels, no glow, no shadow, no gradients.
Avoid: arrows, doors, exit-sign boxes, extra humans, duplicate heads, extra orbit rings, motion streaks, stars, ground lines, textures, text, logos, background patterns. Deliver only the icon on alpha transparency.
